## THE RIGHTS POSITION IS TWO SENTENCES AND THEY DO NOT AGREE

Both sit in the footer of every page, five lines apart, and are quoted here
verbatim because ruling 74 requires the record to quote them and FINDING 69
requires the two properties to be named separately:

```
  "The use of the data of this site with mentioning the source is unrestricted."
  "©2020 Pārsīgdatabase. All Rights Reserved."
```

The Persian footer says the same thing:
`استفاده از داده‌های این سایت با ذکر منبع بلامانع است.`

**This pass does not adjudicate them and no record written from this site may.**
Under ruling 74 the record states both, verbatim, in the order they appear, and
says that the site states two positions. That is what "the rights position is
stated verbatim" means when the source states two.

## What this does to the Persia supply argument

`F6_START.md` made browser pass 2 load-bearing for the Persia floor and said
why: **"every claim in this repository resting on parsigdatabase rests on a
human pass nobody has made."** The claim was that the site refuses. It does not.

`šahrīhā ī ērān` — Šahrestānīhā ī Ērānšahr — is the strongest single candidate
this pass found, and the reason is not that it is the most interesting text on
the site:

- **it is in the window** — a Sasanian-period geography of Ērānšahr, 60
  sections, 110 cities and their founders;
- its edited text is **Jamasp-Asana, *Pahlavi Texts*, 1897–1913, pp. 18–24**,
  which is **public domain** — so the source-text side of the record does not
  rest on the site's two-sentence licence at all;
- the record page carries a **Persian AND an English translation**;
- the record page carries a **full printed bibliography** — Tafazzoli 1368,
  Daryaee 2000-01 and 2002, Gyselen 1988, Marquart/Messina 1931, Modi 1898 and
  1899, Tavadia 1926, Asha 1999 — so a translation_source can be cited by name
  and the boundary of the translation search can be stated rather than guessed;
- the manuscript witness is named on the page: **MK, ff. 19v–26r**.

**THE TEXT ITSELF WAS NOT READ IN THIS PASS.** The locating stopped at the
interface. Neither the transcription tab nor the translation tab was opened, so
a curator's paste is still an independent capture and not a check of this
document against itself. That is the half of the capture rule F5 Part 2 kept and
the half it broke, kept here on purpose.

# Pass 2b — Noor Digital Library, `noorlib.ir`. IT OPENS, AND IT HAS THE MOJMAL.

**Reachable, no login required to browse or search.** Printed on the home page:

```
  کتاب titles       107,857          جلد volumes      149,715
  authors            60,120          publishers        11,612
  text pages     14,614,871          image pages   54,873,089
```

**The site search index is alive and it holds the Mojmal.** Typing
`مجمل التواریخ` into the site's own search box returns an autocomplete list
whose entries include **`مجمل التواریخ و القصص`** — the *Mojmal al-Tawārīkh
wa'l-Qiṣaṣ*. Selecting it returns **two editions, both flagged
`مطالعه رایگان` — free to read** — above a full-text result set across **337
titles**.

**This is not the negative F5 recorded, and the distinction matters.**
`F6_START.md`'s closing sentences say: *"the one that held was the Mojmal's
missing search index, which defeated the person exactly as it defeated the
fetch."* That sentence is about a different site's index. **On `noorlib.ir`
the index is present, the work is in it, and it is free to read.**

## The problem here is rights, not access — the opposite of what was recorded

`noorlib.ir` is a **commercial** digital library: the header carries
`shopping_cart`, `افزایش اعتبار` ("add credit") and `ورود / عضویت`
("log in / register"), and `مطالعه رایگان` is a per-title flag on some works and
not others. **No licence statement was found anywhere reached in this pass.**

Under ruling 74 that is not a bar and it is not a permission. It is the third
condition, and it has a prescribed form: **where a source states no terms the
record says THAT, in those words.** A record built on `noorlib.ir` would carry a
short bounded quotation, the full citation, and the sentence that the source
states no terms — or it is not taken.
