# Data rights

**Every effort has been made to verify that each source is used in line with its rights. Mundus Ulterior makes no copyright claim of its own to any of the source data it uses. If you hold rights in any of that material and have a concern about its use here, please contact Rob Padilla at rob@productofrob.com.**

This file says what the repository holds that is not the project's own, and on
what terms. [`LICENSE.md`](LICENSE.md) says which parts of the repository are
under which license.

## The source corpus

**The project grants no license over the source corpus, or over anything that
quotes it:** `data/seed_corpus/`, the project's records about the corpus in
`data/`, the archived runs and gold set under `eval/`, and the bundle
`ui/data.js` with its manifest. Each source's own terms govern, record by
record. The MIT and CC BY licenses elsewhere in this repository do not reach
any of it.

**Every record's rights position was read off its own source and quoted
verbatim.** They are kept in [`data/corpus_rights.json`](data/corpus_rights.json):
the position, the terms as the source states them, the page they were read off,
the date, and who read them. The table below is a summary. The JSON is the
record. How the readings were made is in
[`docs/RIGHTS_VALIDATION_RESULT.md`](docs/RIGHTS_VALIDATION_RESULT.md) for the
site, and in [`docs/GITHUB_RELEASE_READINGS.md`](docs/GITHUB_RELEASE_READINGS.md)
for this repository.

**Three conditions travel with the corpus:**

- **Non-commercial use.** The project's use is non-commercial research, and
  several sources permit nothing else. Records held as "all rights reserved" or
  "states no terms" are quoted under the project's RULING 74, whose conditions
  are a short quotation bounded by the source, a full citation, the rights
  position stated verbatim in the record, and non-commercial use.
- **ShareAlike.** Text adapted from a ShareAlike source is shared under that
  same license, as the site says.
- **Attribution.** Every record carries its citation. Keep it with the text.

## What the repository carries beyond the records

A repository can be copied whole, which a web page cannot. Five sources ask for
something to travel with every copy, and the repository carries it:

- **`arabia-08-walters-marib-pediment`.** The Walters Art Museum releases its
  longer object descriptions under the GNU Free Documentation License and names
  no version. The record holds one such description entire, so the license
  travels with it: [`LICENSES/GFDL-1.3.txt`](LICENSES/GFDL-1.3.txt).
- **`eastasia-04-jingjiao-stele`.** CBETA's text is "Available for
  non-commercial use when distributed with this header intact." Its header is
  in [`data/rights/cbeta-T54n2144-teiHeader.xml`](data/rights/cbeta-T54n2144-teiHeader.xml),
  exactly as in CBETA's file.
- **`southasia-01-akananuru-149`.** Project Madurai: "You are welcome to freely
  distribute this file, provided this header page is kept intact." The header
  page is in [`data/rights/project-madurai-pm0229-header.txt`](data/rights/project-madurai-pm0229-header.txt).
- **`scandinavia-06-tjurko-bracteate` and `scandinavia-07-rycksta-grikkium`.**
  The Swedish National Heritage Board welcomes reuse of the runic text database
  on web pages, asking that the source be named with a link to the object's
  stable URI on kulturarvsdata.se. Both records give both. That permission is
  the Heritage Board's, and it does not pass to anyone who takes these records
  from this repository.

The museum records are distractors: off-topic texts placed in the search index
so that retrieval has something to get wrong. They are never cited or shown as
evidence.

## What is not here

- **No images of sources.** Records carry text only. Where a museum page routes
  to a separately licensed image, the image's license is recorded in the rights
  cell's note, and the image is not taken.
- **Text withdrawn under the project's own rules.** Greek copied from a site
  whose terms restrict use to personal study was replaced with a public-domain
  edition before launch, and removed from this repository's history before
  publication. [`docs/GITHUB_RELEASE_HISTORY_SCAN.md`](docs/GITHUB_RELEASE_HISTORY_SCAN.md)
  says what, where, and why.
